# General Glossary

| Term                                | Definition                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Administrator                       | Person responsible for managing user accounts and permissions, ensuring that each profile has access only to the functions appropriate to their role. Administrators also configure general system parameters and maintain the integrity of operational data.                                                                                                                                                 |
| Bay                                 | Lengthwise section of the ship.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Berth                               | Ship's allotted place at a wharf or dock.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Cargo                               | Goods carried on a ship.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Cargo Manifests                     | Structured information about the containers involved, including their number, contents (description and cargo type), and positions on the vessel (organized by bays, rows, and tiers).                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Container                           | Reusable, standardized, and durable box, most commonly made of steel, designed to carry goods in ships                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Container Yard                      | Large facility used to store, organize, and manage shipping containers before they are loaded onto a ship.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Crane                               | Large, tall machine used for moving heavy objects by suspending them from a projecting arm or beam.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Crane Operational Capacity          | Measure of average containers per hour, depending on the type of crane (STS cranes usually achieve higher throughput than yard cranes).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Current Availability Status         | Availability of resources. Can be marked as available, unavailable (maintenance), or temporarily out of service                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Dock                                | Area of water in a port for the loading, unloading, and repair of ships.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ETA                                 | Expected time of Arrival.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ETD                                 | Expected time of Departure.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Feeder vessel                       | Small ship serving regional routes and connecting to larger ports.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| HAZMAT                              | Hazardous material.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| IMO number                          | International Maritime Organization number which serves as its international registration and is linked to national or regional maritime authorities.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ISO 6346:2022 standard              | Standard that defines a unique identifier composed of an owner code (three letters), an equipment category identifier (one letter, such as “U” for freight containers), a six-digit serial number, and a single check digit for validation. These globally recognized codes serve as the key reference for all container-related operations, including manifests, yard storage, and vessel loading/unloading. |
| Loading Manifest                    | List of all containers to be loaded onto the vessel for departure. For instance, an entry could indicate container ID CMAU1234567, currently at Yard B, containing electronics, to be loaded at bay 6, row 12, tier 2. This ensures proper sequencing, vessel stability, and efficient resource allocation.                                                                                                   |
| Logistics Operator                  | Person responsible for defining and scheduling operational tasks for approved Vessel Visits, including unloading, loading, and resource allocation (cranes, trucks, yard equipment). Operators monitor the execution of tasks, handle adjustments due to changing operational conditions, and ensure that cargo moves efficiently through docks, yards, and warehouses                                        |
| Mobile resources                    | Yard gantry cranes, trucks or terminal tractors.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Ocean-going container vessels       | Specialized ship designed to transport goods in standardized containers across vast distances on the open ocean.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Panamax vessel                      | Maximum sized vessel that fits through the old Panama Canal locks, typically carrying ~5,000 containers.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Port                                | Harbour or access to navigable water where ships load or unload.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Port Authority Officer              | Person responsible for reviewing and approving or rejecting Vessel Visit notifications submitted by shipping agents, assigning docks to approved visits (potentially with algorithmic support), and overseeing port operations for compliance with safety, environmental, and operational regulations. Officers can also manage shipping agent registrations and vessel authorizations.                       |
| PostPanamax vessel                  | Vessel larger than Panamax vessels, requiring deeper berths and more cranes.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Quay                                | Stone or metal platform lying alongside or projecting into water for loading and unloading ships.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Reefers                             | Refrigerated goods.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Row                                 | Section across the width of the ship.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Setup Time                          | Preparation time, needed by certain resources, before becoming operational. For example, cranes may require calibration, while trucks may need refueling or repositioning.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Ship-to-shore (STS) crane           | Large fixed crane permanently installed at the docks and used to lift containers directly on and off vessels.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Shipping agent                      | Organization that represent vessel owners or operators in port operations. Which primary responsibility is to coordinate administrative and operational tasks associated with a vessel visit.                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Shipping Agent Representative       | Person responsible for submitting Vessel Visit notifications and associated Cargo Manifests for vessels they are authorized to represent. They can update or cancel notifications before approval, provide required documentation, and monitor the status of approved visits.                                                                                                                                 |
| Terminal tractor                    | Vehicle for transporting containers between docks, yards, and warehouses.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| TEU                                 | Twenty-foot Equivalent Unit.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| TEU capacity                        | TEU measurement that determines the maximum capacity of a ship.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Tier                                | Vertical stacks above and below deck.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Truck                               | Large, heavy road vehicle used for carrying goods or materials.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Truck Operational Capacity          | Measure of containers per truck trip and average speed per hour.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Ultra Large Container Vessel (ULCV) | Vessel capable of carrying 18,000+ containers, requiring extensive dock space and simultaneous use of multiple cranes.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Unloading Manifest                  | List of all containers to be offloaded from the vessel upon arrival. For example, a manifest entry might specify container ID MSKU3881445, located at bay 5, row 8, tier 3, containing refrigerated fruit destined for Warehouse A.                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Vessel                              | Ship or large boat.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Vessel visit                        | Planned arrival and departure of a vessel at the port, including associated operations such as cargo loading and unloading. A Vessel Visit may include one cargo manifest (either unloading or loading), two cargo manifests (both unloading and loading), or none (e.g., maintenance calls)                                                                                                                  |
| Vessel visit notification           | Request for a vessel visit made by a shipping agent for an authorized vessel providing key information such as expected arrival (ETA), departure (ETD), cargo type and volume, and any special handling requirements.                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Warehouse                           | Large building where raw materials or manufactured goods may be stored prior to their distribution.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Wharf                               | Area to which a ship may be moored to load and unload.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Yard gantry crane                   | Crane for stacking and moving containers within yards.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
